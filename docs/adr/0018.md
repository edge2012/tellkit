# 0018 — Preserve font licensing

**Context.** Fonts are part of the rendered result and part of the distributed dependencies. Both font families use SIL OFL 1.1, and their license texts travel with the package. Chinese captions and labels make font availability a functional requirement, because a missing glyph can invalidate an otherwise correctly drawn instructional frame.

**Alternatives.** One route replaces fonts whenever redistribution rights or naming requirements are unclear. The other verifies the supplied terms and retains the fonts with their notices, including the renamed subsets. Replacement can simplify an uncertain rights question, but it can also change text metrics and therefore alter wrapping and layout.

**Decision.** Choose the verified OFL fonts with their accompanying license texts. Preserve the renamed subset identities and the original font’s notice when redistributing the assets. Treat the font files and their authorization texts as one dependency unit, so copying a runnable renderer does not accidentally separate the visual assets from their redistribution terms.

**Consequences.** Future font changes require both license review and visual checks for glyph coverage, metrics and line breaks. OFL permission does not imply that every requested character exists in a subset. A package that retains notices can still fail rendering on unsupported text, so licensing checks and glyph validation remain separate responsibilities.
