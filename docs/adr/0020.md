# 0020 — As-is distribution

**Context.** This release provides a bounded rendering example with inspectable inputs and outputs. Its concrete deliverable is a 16-second movie containing 480 frames at 1280×720 and 30 fps. That makes the released behavior reviewable without implying an ongoing service, a staffed support channel or a commitment to future feature work.

**Alternatives.** One route commits to continued maintenance, support and a roadmap for downstream use. The other publishes a fixed version as-is and leaves adaptation to readers. Continued support would require ownership of dependency updates and new use cases; a bounded release instead defines what is available for inspection now.

**Decision.** Choose as-is distribution without a support or roadmap promise. Keep the example runnable with explicit dependency requirements and preserve the decision records that explain its boundaries. Repository hosting settings for issues and pull requests remain an operator responsibility; wording in documentation alone cannot configure how a hosting service accepts contributions.

**Consequences.** Readers own their adaptations, dependency updates and acceptance checks for new content. An external encoder still needs libx264, and bundled font notices still need to accompany redistribution. The absence of future maintenance does not erase those concrete requirements or justify discarding the evidence associated with the version that is being shared.
